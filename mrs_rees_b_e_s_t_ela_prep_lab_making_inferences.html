By Mrs. Rees • Click highlighted clues for teacher insights!

[1] Maya sneezed as she pulled down the rusty attic ladder. Dust motes danced in the pale beam of her flashlight. She stepped carefully across the creaking floorboards, heading toward a dark wooden trunk tucked beneath the eaves.

[2] Resting on top of the trunk sat a brass key attached to a faded blue velvet ribbon, stiff with age. Beside it lay a leather-bound notebook with gold initials stamped on the cover: E.R. 1942.

[3] Maya lifted the heavy lid. Inside, wrapped in yellowed tissue paper, rested a brass compass, a pair of aviator goggles, and a stack of airmail letters stamped with military seals from overseas.

[4] Maya picked up a letter. Her hands trembled slightly as she read the opening line: "My dearest Elizabeth, the skies over London are stormy, but my thoughts stay anchored with you..." She smiled, carefully tucking the letter into her jacket pocket next to her heart.

The Inference Toolkit

✔ Explicit Stated Fact

Information directly written on the page. No reading between the lines required.

Ex: "The trunk had gold initials stamped on the cover: E.R. 1942."

🌟 Sound Inference (Logical Conclusion)

A logical deduction backed strongly by text evidence + your prior knowledge.

Ex: "The trunk belonged to a World War II pilot or service member named E.R."

❌ Wild Guess / Faulty Logic

An assumption not supported by text evidence or contradicting facts.

Ex: "Maya is planning to sell the contents of the trunk on eBay." (No evidence!)
